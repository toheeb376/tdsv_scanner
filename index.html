Attendee register

Required columns: Attendee ID, Full Name, Ticket / Pass Code. Optional: Access Category, Attendee Type / Role, Organisation / Affiliation, Email, Phone, RSVP Status, Check-in Status, Check-in Time, Seat / Section, Notes. IDs and pass codes must be unique.

Reports & backup

Check-ins are stored in this browser only. Export regularly as a backup, and at the end of the event.

The CSV report excludes email, phone and pass codes (data minimisation). The XLSX mirrors the original register columns, with check-in status and time updated.

Reset

Clears every check-in on this device (for example after a rehearsal). The activity log is kept for audit and records the reset.

Privacy & operating notes

  • All data stays on this device. Nothing is sent to any server; there are no analytics or third-party requests.
  • Only the attendee details needed for entry are shown on the scan result. Contact details appear only in the attendee detail view.
  • Restrict access to the device and exported files, and delete them after the retention period agreed for the event (Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023).
  • This browser app keeps its own record of check-ins. Gates on different devices do not sync with each other. Use one device per entrance list, or reconcile exports after the event.
